# Judicial outcomes

Use this reference only to present the dispositions that the governing law and
prepared record make legally available. The accepted outcomes below are a
recognized, non-exhaustive catalogue; their order is not a ranking:

1. **حكم نهائي لصالح المدعي** — final judgment for the claimant.
2. **حكم نهائي لصالح المدعى عليه** — final judgment for the defendant.
3. **حكم تمهيدي بإحالة الدعوى إلى التحقيق** — interlocutory referral for witness evidence.
4. **حكم تمهيدي بندب خبير** — interlocutory appointment of an expert.
5. **قبول الاستئناف شكلاً، وقبل الفصل في الموضوع، بإعادة الدعوى إلى المرافعة ليتناضل الخصوم في موضوعها، وأبقت الفصل في المصروفات لحين صدور حكم مُنهٍ للخصومة** — accept as to form and, before the merits, reopen for further pleadings while reserving costs.
6. **قبول الاستئناف شكلاً، وفي الموضوع، بإعادة الدعوى إلى محكمة أول درجة للفصل في موضوعها** — accept as to form and remand to the Court of First Instance to decide the merits.
7. **حكم تمهيدي بإحالة المحرر المطعون عليه بالتزوير إلى مصلحة الطب الشرعي – قسم أبحاث التزييف والتزوير، ثم إعادة الدعوى إلى المرافعة بعد ورود التقرير ليتناضل الخصوم في شأنه قبل الفصل النهائي** — referral for forgery examination, then reopening after the report before final judgment.
8. **حكم نهائي ينهي الخصومة الاستئنافية دون الفصل في الموضوع، ومن أمثلته الحكم بعدم جواز الاستئناف** — final procedural termination without reaching the merits.

## Build the case-specific options

Explain only the subset the law and current record actually support. Rank that
lawful subset from strongest to weakest legal and evidentiary support; do not
inherit the catalogue order. For every option:

- identify the exact evidence, dispute, and competing interpretation that
  materially supports or weakens it, with durable document and page anchors;
- explain why that record earns the option its stated rank; and
- state the exact factual and legal findings the judge would have to make,
  including any necessary finding about authenticity, coercion, credibility,
  burden of proof, or an evidentiary gap.

For an interlocutory option, identify the exact unresolved matter and explain
how the proposed procedure could resolve it. Never substitute generic legal
elements for the actual record, invent a fact, or force certainty. When options
are genuinely tied or cannot be ranked honestly, say so and identify the
missing or unresolved evidence that causes the tie.

If a lawful available disposition—including a decisive-oath disposition—does not fit the eight, describe it accurately without inventing an ID or forcing a category. Then rank it under the same case-specific rules.

## Keep chat and the record synchronized

Create one substantive Markdown options body. Show that body in full in chat
and write the identical body to the current options file established by the
local-record rules. When the record changes, replace that current file rather
than creating a versioned options file. Keep the decision file separate; it
contains only the judge's direct current selection.

Return the decision to the judge. The agent may identify and explain the
available outcomes but never chooses among them. Record only the judge's direct
current choice, and send any later material change that could affect it back to
the judge for reconsideration or reconfirmation.
