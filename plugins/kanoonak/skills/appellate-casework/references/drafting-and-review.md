# Drafting, citations, and final review

Use this reference for Stages 6 and 7. It is the sole owner of the ruling's
Arabic drafting instruction, citation treatment, placeholders, and final
reread. The DOCX reference owns presentation formatting.

## Draft

> Draft in formal, neutral Egyptian judicial Arabic from the case record,
> governing law, the judge's selected outcome, and the selected approved
> exemplars. Use Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩) for every number in the ruling,
> including dates. Write dates as day/month/four-digit year without leading
> zeros. Mark any genuinely unresolved required content with a
> visible `⟦description⟧` placeholder. Do not invent facts, evidence, authority,
> judicial choices, or missing wording.

The draft must implement the judge's selected outcome and clearly state what
happens to the appealed judgment and whether its reasoning is adopted,
supplemented, or replaced.

Draft the actual structure the ruling requires. Use the selected exemplar to
understand appropriate paragraph roles and judicial style without copying
facts or treating it as a fixed template. Keep allegations attributed and keep
the court's findings distinct from party submissions. The judge's current
choice controls the disposition; never infer, supplement, or silently change
it.

Present every distinct ground of appeal in its own numbered paragraph. For
each ground, state with brevity that preserves its meaning the appellant's
specific complaint, the alleged factual or legal error in the appealed
judgment, and the essential basis supporting the complaint. Never collapse
distinct grounds into a general summary or abbreviate a ground so far that its
substance becomes unclear. This requirement controls when an exemplar groups
or over-compresses the grounds differently. It applies even when only one
ground is stated.

Arabic-Indic digits means Unicode U+0660–U+0669. Write a date such as
`٧/٦/٢٠٢٦`, not `٠٧/٠٦/٢٠٢٦`. This rule also applies to numbers within quotations:
preserve their numerical values and all other quoted text. Leave an unresolved
required value visibly bracketed instead of guessing it.

## Refer to parties

An individual party is a human being—a natural person. An entity party is a legal person, such as a company or public authority. Identify the actual party from the case record, distinguishing an entity from its representative.

Throughout the ruling, refer to individual parties by their actual procedural roles in the relevant proceeding rather than by personal names. Entity parties may be referred to by their verified names or procedural roles.

The following table provides common reference terms:

| English | Arabic |
|---|---|
| Claimant; defendant | المدعي؛ المدعى عليه |
| Appellant; respondent to the appeal | المستأنف؛ المستأنف ضده أو المستأنف عليه |
| Counterclaimant; counterclaim defendant | المدعي فرعياً؛ المدعى عليه فرعياً |
| Intervening party supporting an existing party | المتدخل انضمامياً |
| Intervening party asserting an independent claim | المتدخل هجومياً أو اختصامياً |
| Party brought into the proceedings; party requesting joinder | المُدخَل في الدعوى؛ طالب الإدخال |
| Person bringing a procedural grievance; respondent | المتظلم؛ المتظلم ضده |
| Petitioner for reopening a judgment; respondent | الملتمس؛ الملتمس ضده |
| Party bringing a cassation challenge; respondent | الطاعن؛ المطعون ضده |
| Person raising an execution objection; respondent | المستشكل؛ المستشكل ضده |

Do not use الطالب or المطلوب ضده as generic party designations.

For the contextual description “heirs of the appellants,” use ورثة المستأنفين.

## Treat citations

> Use only legal authorities whose actual text was retrieved and reviewed.
> Before delivery, compare every citation and quotation with that text; omit
> any authority that cannot be checked. The existing citation-verification tool
> may be used when helpful.

`verify_citations` is optional. Do not turn it into a mandatory checker,
separate audit protocol, trust class, fixed report, or substitute for reading
the authority.

## Reread once

> Before delivery, reread the complete draft and correct any inconsistency with
> the case record, governing law, the judge's selected outcome, or the selected
> exemplars.

Apply that instruction to the complete final text after substantive revision.
Correct the draft itself before file creation. Do not add named audit passes,
checker statuses, a repair protocol, digest comparison, preflight form, or
evaluation score.
