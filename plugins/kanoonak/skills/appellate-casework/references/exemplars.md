# Approved exemplars

Use this reference at Stage 5, after the judge has selected the current
outcome. Judicial exemplars guide structure and style; they never supply case
facts, governing law, or the judicial choice.

For the labor workflow, call `get_exemplar_index` and read the complete returned
summary index before selecting exemplars. Select the closest appropriate
approved exemplar or exemplars for the material situation, chosen outcome, and useful drafting
role, respecting every caution in the collection.

For the economic workflow, use its separate economic exemplar-selection
tool to retrieve approved exemplars. If that tool or its approved collection
is unavailable, stop before drafting and explain what is unavailable. Never
substitute the labor collection or treat classified economic candidates as
approved exemplars. The tool contract owns taxonomy and lookup mechanics;
do not invent a tool, taxonomy or lookup contract.

Read every selected exemplar in full and verify its identity. Record each
selection and, when useful, one short reason in the exemplars file established
under the local-record rules. Do not copy the complete collection or full
ruling text into that file. Draft from the case record and reviewed law, using
the verified selected exemplars only for appropriate structure and style.

If the complete labor collection cannot be reviewed, a selected exemplar cannot be
read in full, or its identity cannot be verified, stop before drafting and
explain what is unavailable. Never fall back to an unapproved corpus ruling, a
fixed template, or a removed directive.

The approved collection is server-private data. It is not bundled in this
plugin or written into a public catalogue. Add no client cache, sidecar, index
schema, version, hash, classifier, or resolver.
